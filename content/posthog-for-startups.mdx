Depot is a partner in [PostHog for Startups](https://posthog.com/startups). Approved startups receive a partner code worth $5,000 in Depot credits. The offer is available to new Depot customers only.

## How to redeem the code

1. [Sign up](/sign-up) for a Depot account.
2. Go to your organization [Settings](/orgs/_/settings) page, or click **Configure billing** in the banner at the top of the Depot dashboard.
3. Select a plan.
4. Enter the partner code in the Stripe checkout window.

Stripe checkout shows 100% off the first month and doesn't mention the $5,000. This is expected. Depot grants the credits automatically once you complete checkout.

## How the credits work

- The credits cover your plan (Developer or Startup) and all Depot usage, including container builds, GitHub Actions runners, Depot CI, Depot Registry, and Depot Cache.
- Each invoice draws from the credit balance until it's empty. There are no monthly limits.
- The credits are valid for one year, starting the day you activate the offer at checkout.
- Once the balance is exhausted, Depot charges plan fees and usage overages to the card on file.
